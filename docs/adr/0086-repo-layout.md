# The repo is laid out as its peers are

The repo holds the native app alone, with its parts at the root as its peers keep theirs: `app/`, `engine/`, `tests/`, `third_party/`, `NOTICE`, and `docs/`. Both were the user's calls once the Electron app was retired (ADR 0051): the repo cleaned of what the Electron app left, then a layout chosen after a look at 15 peers.

The Electron-era docs left the tree: 40 plans and specs (`docs/superpowers`), the extension-support doc, and the parity matrix and cutover report, whose open rows the checklist (`docs/human-checklist.md`) carries. ADRs 0001 and 0002 now say how the native app does what they decided (`NSApp.appearance`; PaneKit's `Updates`), and 0003 stays as the record 0004 supersedes. Lint settings for code the repo no longer has (Tailwind classes, JSX, CSS selectors, decorators) and the web build's ignores are gone. The `native-shell` branch is retired, its work all on `main`, and CI runs on `main` and pull requests.

`native/` came from the months the native app lived beside the Electron one; with the Electron app gone, it wrapped the whole repo, and no peer wraps itself so. Ardia, the nearest (an Arc-like Chromium browser for the Mac, whose prebuilt-engine model Pane follows, ADR 0049), has `engine/`, `apps/`, `docs/`, `scripts/`, and `third_party/` at its root; Ghostty keeps its core and its Mac app side by side (`src/`, `macos/`); Electron, Brave, and CEF keep their patches, docs, and tests at the root, as ungoogled-chromium and Helium keep `patches/` and `docs/`. So `app/`, `engine/`, `tests/`, `third_party/`, `NOTICE`, and `docs/` (`building.md`, `human-checklist.md`, and the ADRs) are at the root. The scenarios left `engine/` for `tests/`: they drive the whole app. The app's workflow is `.github/workflows/app.yml`. Decisions are recorded as ADRs in `docs/adr/`, one per decision, and the single log that held them until 2026-10-28 (`docs/decisions.md`) is in git history.

The engine key did not change: it hashes the git trees of the engine's sources, which moved but did not change, so `engine-0d0a01f8c703` still serves. The engine's sources and a patch keep `native/` in 13 comments until the next engine change: an edit there, even to a comment, changes the key and needs a new engine. `engine/scripts/update-exports.sh` keeps writing the old path into the patch it makes, in step with it.

The move was checked with lint, typecheck, knip, PaneKit's 194 tests, a clean build and package, `dist.sh`, and `smoke`, `migration`, `updates`, `sidebar-scroll`, and `progress-bar` on that build. The Chromium checkout's `src/pane` links to `engine/pane` now.
