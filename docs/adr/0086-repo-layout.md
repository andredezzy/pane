# The repo is laid out as its peers are

The repo holds Pane alone, with its parts at the root as its peers keep theirs: `pane/`, the `//pane` tree, Pane's code; `chromium/`, what Pane does to Chromium (the pinned tag, its args, its patches, the engine archives' keys, and `UPGRADING.md`); `scripts/`; `packaging/`, what packaging reads (`VERSION`, the icon, the dmg's resources, the entitlements); `tests/`; `third_party/` and `NOTICE`; and `docs/` (`building.md`, `human-checklist.md`, the ADRs, `benchmarks/`, `qa/`, and `growth/`). `docs/building.md` names what each holds. Decisions are ADRs in `docs/adr/`, one per subject. A plan lives in `docs/plans/` while it runs, and its last commit removes it: what it decided lives in the ADRs, and the rest in git history.

Ardia, the nearest peer (an Arc-like Chromium browser for the Mac, whose prebuilt-engine model Pane follows, ADR 0049), has `engine/`, `apps/`, `docs/`, `scripts/`, and `third_party/` at its root; Ghostty keeps its core and its Mac app side by side (`src/`, `macos/`); Electron, Brave, and CEF keep their patches, docs, and tests at the root, as ungoogled-chromium and Helium keep `patches/` and `docs/`. The layout was the user's call once the Electron app was retired (ADR 0051), after a look at 15 peers, and it has moved twice since, each move in one commit:

- **Out of `native/` (2026-10-28).** `native/` came from the months the native app lived beside the Electron one; with the Electron app gone, it wrapped the whole repo, and no peer wraps itself so. It became `app/` (the Swift app), `engine/` (the engine's sources, patches, and scripts), `tests/`, `third_party/`, `NOTICE`, and `docs/`: the scenarios left `engine/` for `tests/`, since they drive the whole app. The repo was cleaned of what the Electron app left: 40 plans and specs (`docs/superpowers`), the extension-support doc, and the parity matrix and cutover report, whose open rows went to the checklist; lint settings for code it no longer had (Tailwind classes, JSX, CSS selectors, decorators) and the web build's ignores; and the `native-shell` branch, its work all on `main`. The single log that held the decisions until then (`docs/decisions.md`) is in git history. Checked with lint, typecheck, knip, PaneKit's 194 tests, a clean build and package, `dist.sh`, and `smoke`, `migration`, `updates`, `sidebar-scroll`, and `progress-bar` on that build.
- **For the move to Views (2026-11-08 and 2026-11-11,** the user's D1 and D2, ADR 0124). With the UI moving into Chromium, `engine/` no longer named anything apart from the app, so it went before any new file did: `engine/pane` is `pane/`; `engine/CHROMIUM_VERSION`, `args/`, `patches/`, `UPGRADING.md`, and `prebuilt-engines.tsv` are in `chromium/`; and `engine/scripts` is `scripts/`. The App workflow went with the Swift app (ADR 0049), and once the Swift app, PaneKit, and the watchdog's Swift had gone, `app/`'s remains became `packaging/`, its paths in CI, the scripts, `pane/BUILD.gn`, and ADR 0044 renamed with it. Checked with `dist.sh`, which packaged the app and built its dmg from the new paths, which `update-install` installed, and lint, knip, and the OS-flag check.

Neither move changed the engine key: it hashes the git trees of the engine's sources, which moved but did not change, so `engine-0d0a01f8c703` served through the first, and the key was `b8eb8b0e` before and after the second. The Chromium checkout's `src/pane` links to `pane`.
