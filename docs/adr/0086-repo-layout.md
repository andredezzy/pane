# The repo is laid out as its peers are

> **Changed by the move to Views (the user, 2026-11-08, D1 and D2 in `docs/plans/views-migration.md`):** the App workflow is gone (ADR 0049), and `engine/` is gone: `pane/`, `chromium/`, and `scripts/` sit at the root, as the last paragraph says.

> **On the Views line** (`docs/plans/views-migration.md`, K3): the Swift app, PaneKit, and the watchdog's Swift left `app/`, which keeps what packaging reads: `VERSION`, the icon (`icon/`, `AppIcon.icon` with its renders and `make-icons.swift`), the dmg's resources, the entitlements, and the build's caches (`build/`, `release/`). The last paragraph's move of them to `packaging/` is not made: it would rename paths that CI, the scripts, and ADR 0044 name, for nothing they do differently, so it waits for the user's word.

The repo holds the native app alone, with its parts at the root as its peers keep theirs: `app/`, `engine/`, `tests/`, `third_party/`, `NOTICE`, and `docs/`. Both were the user's calls once the Electron app was retired (ADR 0051): the repo cleaned of what the Electron app left, then a layout chosen after a look at 15 peers.

The Electron-era docs left the tree: 40 plans and specs (`docs/superpowers`), the extension-support doc, and the parity matrix and cutover report, whose open rows went to the checklist (`docs/human-checklist.md`). ADRs 0001 and 0002 now say how the native app does what they decided (`NSApp.appearance`; PaneKit's `Updates`), and the Electron-era record that ADR 0004 superseded stayed (it is folded into 0004 now). Lint settings for code the repo no longer has (Tailwind classes, JSX, CSS selectors, decorators) and the web build's ignores are gone. The `native-shell` branch is retired, its work all on `main`, and CI runs on `main` and pull requests.

`native/` came from the months the native app lived beside the Electron one; with the Electron app gone, it wrapped the whole repo, and no peer wraps itself so. Ardia, the nearest (an Arc-like Chromium browser for the Mac, whose prebuilt-engine model Pane follows, ADR 0049), has `engine/`, `apps/`, `docs/`, `scripts/`, and `third_party/` at its root; Ghostty keeps its core and its Mac app side by side (`src/`, `macos/`); Electron, Brave, and CEF keep their patches, docs, and tests at the root, as ungoogled-chromium and Helium keep `patches/` and `docs/`. So `app/`, `engine/`, `tests/`, `third_party/`, `NOTICE`, and `docs/` (`building.md`, `human-checklist.md`, and the ADRs, joined since by `benchmarks/` and `growth/`) are at the root. The scenarios left `engine/` for `tests/`: they drive the whole app. The app's workflow is `.github/workflows/app.yml`. Decisions are recorded as ADRs in `docs/adr/`, one per subject, and the single log that held them until 2026-10-28 (`docs/decisions.md`) is in git history.

The engine key did not change: it hashes the git trees of the engine's sources, which moved but did not change, so `engine-0d0a01f8c703` still serves. The engine's sources and a patch keep `native/` in 13 comments (11 files of `pane/browser/ui`, and patch 0001): an edit there, even to a comment, changes the key and needs a new engine, and the engine changes since have left them. `scripts/update-exports.sh` keeps writing the old path into the patch it makes, in step with it.

The move was checked with lint, typecheck, knip, PaneKit's 194 tests, a clean build and package, `dist.sh`, and `smoke`, `migration`, `updates`, `sidebar-scroll`, and `progress-bar` on that build. The Chromium checkout's `src/pane` links to `pane` now.

**The tree, for the move to Views (2026-11-08).** With the UI moving into Chromium, `engine/` no longer named anything apart from the app, so it went before any new file did, in one commit, so nothing moves twice: `engine/pane` is `pane/`, the `//pane` tree; `engine/CHROMIUM_VERSION`, `args/`, `patches/`, `UPGRADING.md`, and `prebuilt-engines.tsv` are in `chromium/`, what Pane does to Chromium; and `engine/scripts` is `scripts/`. `app/`'s remains go to `packaging/` as the Swift goes. The engine key hashes the same git trees, so the move kept it (`b8eb8b0e` before and after), and the files it hashes were left byte for byte, a path they name waiting for their next change.
