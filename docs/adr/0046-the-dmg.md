# The dmg is built with dmgbuild, LZMA-compressed, and laid out as Hark's installer, its volume icon on the title's centre line

**The build.** Pane's dmg is built with dmgbuild, as electron-builder built the Electron app's. Without it, laying out the window would mean scripting Finder, which asks for Automation permission, or writing `.DS_Store` files by hand. `scripts/dist.sh` packages the app stripped (ADR 0047), refuses one without Chrome's CRLSet (ADR 0040), and writes `app/release/Pane-<version>-arm64.dmg`, the name the update check looks for in a GitHub release (ADR 0002), with the app's symbols beside it. It runs the same dmgbuild that electron-builder 26.8.1 downloads, dmgbuild 1.6.7 in a standalone Python, pinned by its SHA-256, and keeps the Electron dmg's window place: 400 points from the left, centered on a 1440-point screen height. `PANE_DMG_OUT` puts a dmg anywhere, as `PANE_APP_OUT` does the app for `package.sh`, so a dmg to install from leaves the release's in `app/release` as it is. The user installs the native app by hand from such a dmg.

**The format.** The dmg is ULMO (LZMA) since 0.13.0, where it was the Electron dmg's UDZO (zlib) at level 9. Every user downloads it, at install and at every update, and LZMA makes it 22% smaller: 145.7 MB against 187.6 MB for 0.12.0, 42 MB less each time, about 6.7 s at 50 Mbit/s. Unpacking costs more: copying the app out of a mounted ULMO dmg took 2.80 s against 0.70 s, which an update spends in the background while staging (ADR 0092). macOS reads ULMO since 10.15, and Pane needs 14 (ADR 0085). The reverse engineering of Aside's and Dia's bundles found it (2026-10-30).

**The window** copies Hark's installer since 0.11.1, at the user's request ("identical"), and carries Pane's own icon, wordmark, and arrow. A hazy sky runs from blue at the top to pale gray at the bottom, its colours sampled from the user's screenshot of Hark's. "Install **Pane**" sits above in the wordmark's type, Funnel Display Regular with the name in Medium (ADR 0099), 32 points. "Drag the app onto the Applications folder" sits under it in the system font, 18 points, as Finder's labels are. A hand-drawn arrow runs between Pane and the Applications link. The icons are 128 points, 215 and 518 points in and 284 down, with 16-point labels. The window is 720 × 560 points under Finder's 32-point title bar: dmgbuild's size is the whole window, so `dist.sh` adds the title bar. The volume, so the title, is "Pane Installer", as Hark's is "Hark Installer". The updater mounts the dmg at a path of its own (`UpdateInstaller`), so the name is the window's alone.

It was designed in Paper first (08 · Installer › 08.1 Installer window). The arrow is A10 of ten drawn in the whole window (08.2 Arrow options). `app/dmg/make-background.swift` draws it into `app/dmg/background.tiff`, at 1x and 2x, tagged sRGB. The sky mixes its stops in Oklab, as Paper does, and its haze is Paper's eight soft ellipses. The headline is drawn from `app/dmg/fonts/FunnelDisplay[wght].ttf`, registered for the script alone; it stops if the font does not load, so no fallback is ever drawn. The dmg carries the image, not the font, and the image is Pane's own, not electron-builder's arrow (`NOTICE`). It runs 40 points taller than the window, the sky's last colour alone, so a shorter title bar on another macOS leaves no gap.

From 0.7.0 to 0.11.0 the window was Dia's installer in Pane's neutral colors (the user's request then; Paper 08.3, retired): "To install, drag **Pane** to **Applications**" above Pane and the Applications link, 200-point icons 420 points apart with three chevrons between them, on a white-to-gray wash, 800 × 412 points, with the volume named "Pane". Hark's replaced it at the user's request.

**The volume icon** is Pane's icon with its 16-point art 1.5 points lower, so Finder's title bar shows it on the title's centre line: the user's call, on the installer's window, where the icon sat above the title. Finder sets the volume's icon there itself, at 16 points, with its centre 1.5 points above the title's (from its capitals' tops to its baseline): 14.75 points down against 16.25, measured on the user's screenshot and in a Finder window here. Dia's installer sits 1 point off the same way; Pane's icon, a white frame around dark glass, shows it more. `app/dmg/make-volume-icon.swift` makes `app/dmg/VolumeIcon.icns` from macOS's own drawing of Day (`AppIconDay`, ADR 0089): its 16-point images lowered, the 32-, 128-, and 256-point ones as drawn, for the desktop and Get Info, which show the icon alone. `dist.sh` gives it to dmgbuild in place of the app's `AppIcon.icns`. It was measured on macOS 27. A Finder that centres the icon itself would show it 1.5 points low: re-measure, and set `lowered` in the script to 0.

What was rejected:

- **`Install Pane.command`, which the Electron dmg carried:** it copied the app and cleared its quarantine, for builds without notarization, but a dmg downloaded in a browser quarantines the script too, and Gatekeeper blocks it as it blocks the app. A local build is not quarantined, and neither is the dmg the in-app update downloads once it checks out (ADR 0002).
- **UDZO, as before:** 42 MB more for every download, to save about 2 s of background work.
- **ULFO (LZFSE):** about as fast as UDZO to unpack, and barely smaller (185.4 MB).
- **A green cartoon mountain behind the icons:** the user's first request for the Hark-era window, drawn in code and then generated in Paper; the user turned down both and asked for Hark's window as it is.
- **A photograph of a blurred sky:** Paper's gradients come within 3 levels of Hark's sampled colours, with no image to source or license.
- **Funnel Display for the subtitle:** the user's call; it stays in the system font.
- **The other nine arrows:** Hark's own, a straight one, a chevron, three chevrons, a dotted path, an arc, a filled one, one in a disc, and a long hairline (08.2).
- **Keeping "Pane" as the volume name:** Hark's window says "Hark Installer"; nothing reads the name.
- **Moving the title, for the volume icon:** Finder draws it.
- **Lowering every size of the volume icon:** where the icon stands alone, it is centred as drawn.
- **Another volume icon, a disk:** the installer carries Pane's icon, as Dia's carries Dia's.

How it is checked:

- **Finder:** a test dmg from `dist.sh` (`PANE_SKIP_PACKAGE=1 PANE_DMG_OUT=...`), mounted and opened as a user's would be, its window captured alone, against Paper's 08.1 at 2x: every sampled point of the sky is within 3 levels in sRGB. Finder draws the labels 20.5 points higher than the board first did; the board now draws them where Finder does.
- **Dark mode:** with the Mac set to dark, Finder darkens the title bar alone. It draws the labels dark on the light sky, as in light mode, so they read the same. A dmg has one background for both, as Hark's does.
- **The font:** `make-background.swift` checks that Funnel Display loaded before it draws.
- **The volume icon:** two test dmgs laid out as the release's, one with the app's icon and one with the new, were mounted and opened. The icon's centre sat 1.50 points above the title's with the app's icon, and 0.00 with the new one. A dmg from `dist.sh` carries a `.VolumeIcon.icns` identical to `app/dmg/VolumeIcon.icns`. Finder's sidebar draws a volume as a monochrome disk, so the change shows in the title bar and in 16-point lists alone. Paper's 08.1 shows the Day icon, in the title bar and as Pane.
- **The format:** the 0.12.0 dmg converted with hdiutil into each format, attached, and its app copied twice (2026-10-30). The next release's dmg is ULMO (`hdiutil imageinfo`), and the live update check against it passes: download, staging, the swap, and the reopen.
