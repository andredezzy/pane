# Views redraw only what changed and never mask the page

The app's views redraw only what changed, and nothing the app draws masks the page or animates it in, so the page keeps its own compositing and a covered page costs nothing. Three changes made it so: the sidebar's rows take values, nothing clips the page, and a page under Settings or Extensions is hidden, with no fade on a tab switch.

The sidebar's sections and rows take what they show as values and compare them (`.equatable()`), so SwiftUI runs only the changed ones. A profile section had taken an edit closure SwiftUI cannot compare, and every tab row read the model's selection itself. Counted with each body logging itself, selecting a tab in another profile, of 24 tabs in three, ran 24 tab rows and 3 sections, and now runs 2 and 2; a tab navigating ran 6 sections, and now runs 2. The active profile's bar still morphs (16, 13.5, 5.5, then 4 points across screen captures; ADR 0064).

Nothing masks the page. `RootView` clipped the panel, page included, to its squircle, and SwiftUI did it by hosting the page in an `_NSGraphicsView` with a `MaskLayer`: the per-frame mask the page's own rounded layer was there to avoid. Read from the layer tree with a probe build, that mask is gone, and the hosting view's masked sublayers went from 16 to 4. Only what SwiftUI draws over the page (Settings, Extensions, the empty state, the sad tab) is clipped, to the panel's corners (`PanelCorners`, whose top corners meet the toolbar square). The page's layer takes its radius from the panel, so it is square in full screen, as the panel is; that was not checked on screen, since the full screen scenario brings the app forward. Captured from the screen, the panel's edge pixels differ by at most 14 of 255.

The page under Settings and Extensions is hidden. It kept drawing under the surfaces that cover it, and hidden in place it stayed visible to the engine as the window's active tab. Now the selected tab's page is taken out of the panel and hidden, as a tab switch does the old one (ADR 0011); the `showPage` intent does it (ADR 0078). Over an animating page under Settings, the page drew 480 frames in 4 seconds before, and 0 now; the GPU helper's CPU went from 0.24 seconds in 5 to 0.03, and the renderer's from 0.31 to 0. `navigation` checks the page hidden under both and visible after; it failed before.

A tab no longer fades in. A tab switch faded its page in over about a quarter second (its background at 172, 241, then 244 of 255 across screen captures): a selection that animated, against DESIGN.md › Motion, from Core Animation's implicit actions on the views going in and out. The page now goes in and out with them off, and shows fully at the first capture, after a switch and back from Settings.
