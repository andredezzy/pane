# Pane on Windows looks as Paper's page 11 draws it: its own caption buttons in the panel's corner, the menu bar's commands under "Pane ⌄", and Windows' words

Pane's window on Windows is the Mac's, with what Windows does its own way drawn in Paper first (ADR 0058, page "11 · Windows" and the Windows boards beside 05, 06, and 07) and chosen by the user one by one on 2026-11-13, then built (W5, 2026-11-14; `evidence/views-migration/runs/p5-w5i-notes.md`):

- **The caption buttons at the toolbar's end, in the content panel's top-right corner** (11.1's B), Chrome's own place where no tab strip runs across the top: no band over the panel, the sidebar's header and the frame around the panel moving the window. Minimize, Maximize or Restore, and Close, 45 by 42 and 1 apart, Chrome's `Windows11IconPainter` glyphs, black on Pane's light panel and white on its dark one (`pane/ui/window/window_controls_win.cc`). Under the PIN screen, in Settings, and with no tab, where no toolbar shows, they sit in that same corner, over the PIN screen's panel while it shows (07.x, 06.4.1).
- **Their states as Chrome's `WindowsCaptionButton`** (11.2): the glyph's color at 10% under the pointer, fading in and out on Pane's hover (`motion::Hover`), 20% pressed, at once; Close's #E81123 with a white X, pressed at 0x98; an inactive window's glyphs at 40% but under the pointer; Close's fill rounded by the panel's corner; a maximized window's Close reaching the frame's gutter above and beside it, so the screen's corner pixel presses it.
- **Snap Layouts from Maximize** (11.3): the buttons answer Windows' hit test as its own (HTMINBUTTON, HTMAXBUTTON, HTCLOSE, `RootView::NonClientHitTest`), so hovering Maximize opens Windows' flyout, which Pane draws nothing of; Maximize stays lit under it while the pointer is on it.
- **The frame** is the system's without its title bar or controls, as Chrome's browser window has it there (`frame_win.cc`'s `TreeHost`: `SYSTEM_DRAWN_NO_CONTROLS`, the client area running to the window's top, the frame keeping its sides, bottom, Windows 11's 8-point corners, hairline, and shadow), its top 5 points resizing the window (Chrome's restored top border) and its 16-point corners diagonally. The window opens with what it shows 1280 by 800, as on the Mac, the resize edges around it. The backdrop is solid, Pane's own colors (11.9).
- **The menu bar's commands under "Pane ⌄"** in the sidebar's header (11.4's A, 11.5), where the Mac's header names Pane after the traffic lights: one Views menu as Chrome draws its own on Windows, Pane's own items first, then File, Edit, View, Navigate, Tab, and Window as submenus, then Exit Pane (11.5.1), each item's state asked of the app as the Mac's menu bar asks it, from the one command table (ADR 0019). A press opens it 2 under the button, its look held while it shows; Alt alone and F10 open it from the keys, as they open Chrome's (`AppMenuKeys`), and the button is no Tab stop, as Chrome's own menu button isn't. From it Pane runs the platform's own commands itself (`platform_commands_win.cc`): the Edit menu on what has the keys, Minimize, and Maximize.
- **Windows' words** (11.6, 11.7): Exit wherever the Mac says Quit and it shows there: Exit Pane, Exit Pane? and its Exit, the lock's 07.6 and 07.8 lines, its update action (07.9), and Exit and install; "PC" for the Mac (Settings' Lock after, the profile sheet's lines, 05.5.1 and 06.4.1); keys as Windows writes them on the keyed chips: Enter for ↩, Alt+F4 on Exit Pane?'s (`pane/ui/design/system_words_{mac,win}.cc`).
- **The lock's lines on Windows** for the chip's own states (07.10's A for the two asked): its lockout (07.1.1), a new PIN it wouldn't take (07.3.1), its key gone (07.6.1), the chip off (07.6.2), and DPAPI refusing the secret (07.7.1) (`AppLock::System::Words`, from `data_launch_win.cc`; ADR 0113).
- **Windows 11's overlay scroll bars** (11.9's A): a 2-point line over the content at rest, a 6-point thumb on a faint track with arrows under the pointer, gone once nothing moves, taking nothing from the rows (`scroll_win.cc`).
- **Contrast themes followed** (11.10): while one is on, each of Pane's colors takes one of its roles (`AddForcedColors`, `palette_win.cc`), a fill that told surfaces apart gives way to a 1-point edge (`kPaneColorForcedEdge`: a control's, "Pane ⌄"'s at rest, and the point between the toolbar and the page), the active profile's row loses its tint, a sleeping tab takes GrayText (`kPaneColorForcedGrayText`), shadows and veils go, the focus ring and a hovered caption button take Highlight, with no red for Close, and a profile's color stays, edged. Every view paints again at each change, as at any theme's (ADR 0001), so a view on a layer of its own takes the colors, which a contrast theme gives after its switch, in more than one change.
- **The taskbar's icon** is the Dock's image as it is (11.11's B), the App icon setting's sky, Automatic following Pane's theme as on the Mac (ADR 0089).

## Chosen with the test VM's renders, each behind one constant in its platform file

The user's calls of 2026-11-14, each one line to change:

- **Segoe UI Variable, Windows 11's own UI font, for every text** (11.8's C; `kMediumOnWindows` in `pane/ui/design/text_win.cc`): Skia makes each size and weight from the system's own font, its Text face with its axes set (`SkTypeface::makeClone`), so Medium is 500, which Segoe UI has no face for, and its optical size is the text's size from 8 to 36, as Windows gives it ("the optical size will automatically match the requested font-size", Microsoft's Typography in Windows 11). Windows 10, which has no Variable, takes Segoe UI, its Regular in Medium's place: the user found Semibold, what DirectWrite matches 500 to, bad. In the test VM it moved the launch past nothing but the VM's own noise. Rejected: Semibold (A), Regular (B), and Inter bundled beside `chrome.dll` (D, its code since gone).
- **ClearType for Pane's labels** (`kClearTypeLabels`, `text_win.cc`), on the opaque ground each sits on: a surface names its ground (`SetGround`, `pane/ui/design/ground.h`: the window's color under the sidebar, the panel's card under the toolbar, Settings, the PIN screen, the empty and crashed pages; a dialog's or the sheet's card), a control's look over it as lit and pressed as it is (`ControlHighlight`, `GroundUnderControl`) and what a row draws of its own (a profile's tint), blended by Skia itself to the bit; and the label paints that ground under its text in its own layer, which ClearType blends each glyph's channels with, where a transparent layer holds nothing (`SetLabelGround`). Text with no opaque ground stays grayscale: the update pill's, which crossfades in layers of its own, a dragged row's copy, the key chips on their rounded chips, the full screen's hint, and text fields.
- **Alt+F4 alone for Exit** (`kCtrlQExitsOnWindows`, `pane/ui/app/commands.cc`, now false): Ctrl+Q runs nothing on Windows, as in Chrome, Edge, Dia, and Arc; Exit Pane? still asks first (ADR 0053).
- **Tab as in Chrome** (ADR 0019): the toolbar's and the sidebar's buttons take no Tab on Windows; F6, Shift+F6, Alt+Shift+T, and the arrows reach them (`pane/ui/design/tab_order.h`, `pane/ui/window/focus_groups.h`).

## What was rejected

- **A 32-point band for the caption buttons over the panel** (11.1's A), Microsoft's own guidance: the user chose the toolbar's end. **The buttons top left, in the lights' order** (11.1's C): no Windows app keeps them there.
- **A ⋯ button at the toolbar's end** for the commands (11.4's B), and **Windows' Alt menu bar** (11.4's C).
- **Quit, everywhere** (11.7's B): Windows' apps exit.
- **Mica or Acrylic** behind the sidebar: the user may ask for it later. **Pane's Mac scroller on both systems**, and **Views' classic scroll bar** (11.9's B and C).
- **The icon cut to Windows' grid** (11.11's A): new art, the user's call.

## How it is checked

- **Against the boards,** in the test VM at 100%, light and dark: each state rendered from the build and captured from the VM's screen beside its board (`p5-w5i-notes.md`).
- **The scenarios,** on both systems: `window-chrome` and `fullscreen` read the caption buttons where the Mac's lights are read (`trafficLightsInPlace` by system), `window-drag` the frame's gutters and Windows' top edge, `sidebar-resize` and `extension-shortcuts` the window's 1280 points, `tab-focus` and `updates` the app menu through the channel by the Mac's menus' paths (`HAS_MENU_BAR`), `security` the menu behind the lock.
- **`pane_unittests`:** the command table's Windows column (`CommandsTest.WindowsTakesCtrlAndItsOwnKeys`, `ShownKeys`, Ctrl+Q running nothing in `EachKeyRunsOneCommand`), the lock's words per state (`AppLockGuardTest`), the palette's every id, buttons out of Tab and their order (`TabOrderTest`).
- **ClearType's grounds,** on each state's render in the VM, light and dark: the text fringed, and no box around it, a ground a step off showing as one (`p5-w5i-notes.md`).
- **`focus-groups`,** on both systems: on Windows F6 along the address, the toolbar's first button, the sidebar's, and the page, Shift+F6 back, the arrows within, Esc out, Alt+Shift+T, and Shift+Tab from the address landing on no button; on the Mac, the keys left where they were.
