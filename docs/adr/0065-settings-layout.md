# Settings take Aside's layout

Settings are laid out as Aside's Appearance page, at the user's request: a title, then each section as a card of rows, each row with its title, a muted description, and its control at the end. Aside's page was measured at 2× from the user's screenshot and set at Pane's type scale, a point under Aside's 14. Paper drew it first (06.1 Settings, light and dark; ADR 0058).

Settings (`pane/ui/panels/settings_view.cc`) shows in the content panel, in the page's place (ADR 0073). The page has "Settings" at 22, then each section's muted label at 13 over a card, the cards 40 in and at most 640 wide. Cards are rounded 22 with a 1-point border, and their rows are at least 56 points tall, with a hairline between. A row's title is 13 and its description 12, muted, and its control sits 11 points from the card's edge. The version is the Updates card's last row, drawn without tabular digits (Paper 10.6, ADR 0060).

**The presets are pop-up buttons,** as Aside's are: 32 points, with a 1-point border and no fill (ADR 0063), showing the value (Theme's with its icon) and up and down chevrons, and opening the system's menu with the current option checked, just under the button as the press goes down. They are `MenuButton`s (`pane/ui/design/menu_button.h`), the app's one menu trigger (ADR 0070), which the profile sheet's time zone and proxy type use too (ADR 0120), and VoiceOver reads their option as their value (ADR 0071). The PIN and update buttons are the outline buttons, small.

**The menu shows under its button, on the Mac too.** In the front session of 2026-11-12 the menu opened at the click, not under the button, and past the window's right edge: `views::MenuRunner`'s Mac runner places a context menu at the current press over the anchor it is given (`MenuRunnerImplCocoa`, `ui/base/cocoa/menu_utils.mm`), so only a menu opened without a press showed under its button. `MenuButton` runs `AnchoredMenu` (`pane/ui/design/anchored_menu.h`): on the Mac that runner's own pieces, `MenuControllerCocoa` and `ui::ShowContextMenu`, with the press moved to the anchor and nothing else of it changed, so the menu, its look, and its tasks held are as before; on Windows `views::MenuRunner`, which honors the anchor. No Chromium patch. A moved press keeps its release: a context menu's opening press records no place in AppKit's tracking (`NSMenuTrackingSession`, whose drag threshold measures from a press it records), so the release is judged by time alone, as before. Rejected: a patch to `MenuRunnerImplCocoa` (no Chromium patches for now), and `-[NSMenu popUpMenuPositioningItem:atLocation:inView:]`, which Chromium avoids for menus drawn too narrow (`web_menu_runner_mac.mm`).

The Electron app's caps labels and the hairlines between sections are gone. Extensions left Settings for a page of their own (ADR 0062), and Security follows Memory & disk. The Swift app drew Settings to the same measures, its pop-up buttons its `PaneMenuButton`, until 0.20.0.

Checked by `pane_unittests` (`AnchoredMenuTest`, `AnchoredMenuMacTest`: a press and a key show the menu at the anchor, and closing tells the trigger) and, in front, by `checkMenuUnderTrigger` in `tests/rigs/views-profile-sheet.ts` and `views-settings.ts`; `tests/rigs/views-settings.ts` renders Settings' states.
