# The find bar takes Aside's design

The find bar takes Aside's design, at the user's request, measured at 2× from the user's screenshot, without Aside's blue line between the field and the chevrons (the user's call). Paper drew it first: 04.3 Find in page, light and dark, and 03.5's three states (ADR 0058).

The bar (`pane/ui/page/find_bar_view`) is 357 by 54 points, at the window's top right (64 points down, 24 in), on the popover's fill. Aside rounds it as a circle of 8, which is 14.7 as a squircle; the bar's squircle was 14 at first and is 15 now, on the radius scale (ADR 0057). Its soft shadow (black 25%, 40% in dark; radius 6, 6 down) matched Aside's across its edge and spread, within a pixel or two. It has no border, but in dark a hairline ring, as the popovers have, so it stands off the page.

Inside, the field starts 16 points in, at 13, a point under Aside's 14, as Settings took it (ADR 0065). It shows no placeholder, as Aside's shows none, though one stays, unseen, for VoiceOver. Then come the count (12, red when nothing matches, its digits in cells of one width so it doesn't shift as it changes, `DigitCellsLabel`, Paper 10.6 and ADR 0060) and the chevrons, 4 apart (18 points, Lucide's stroke), and last close (10 points, a 2.5 stroke), each in a 32-point circle with the accent's fill on hover. The chevrons and close land on Aside's to the pixel. Disabled is 40%, as Aside's gray, the icon buttons' one style (`IconButton`, ADR 0070): the Swift app's plain style had dimmed a disabled label a second time. The caret is the text's color, not the system's blue.

**A find session is the tab's, as in Chrome.** The bar is a plain view of Pane's in the window, the FindBar of Chrome's own `FindBarController` (`pane/ui/page/find_in_page.h`), not Chrome's child widget, so ⌘F, the tab's find session, and its results are Chrome's: the bar comes back with its tab and closes as the page goes to another, where the Swift app's stayed over the window. What showed stays Pane's: it opens empty, never filled from the page's selection or the system's find text, an emptied field keeps the page's selection, and closing empties it; Pane puts nothing of its own on the system's find pasteboard. The controller's Mac helper brings one Chrome behavior with it: a find text another app set, which Pane reads as it comes forward, is taken by a bar that shows, field and search alike. Esc, close, or a press anywhere else in the window closes it, as the Swift app's overlay did.

⌘G and ⇧⌘G (View › Find Next and Find Previous) step to the next match and the previous one, as Return and ⇧Return do in the field, which takes the keys, as Chrome's `FindBarController::Show` does for them: the user's call (2026-11-10), from Chrome and every Mac app, where the Swift had neither. With the bar closed, ⌘G opens it as ⌘F does, empty, so it finds nothing until a text is typed. `find` steps with both keys through the menu bar.

The Electron app's bar is gone: rounded-lg with a border and shadow-md, a 176-point field, and arrows. `mediumShadow`, `arrowUp`, and `arrowDown` had no other use, and went with it.
