# A tab is loading only while its main frame loads a new document, and the address bar shows the selected tab's load as Chrome's toolbar progress bar

A tab counts as loading only while its main frame loads a new document, Chrome's `ShouldShowLoadingUI`, and the address bar's load progress is a port of the bar stable Chrome draws, showing the selected tab's own load alone. The user reported that switching to a tab long loaded showed the address bar's progress for a few seconds, and their call was to reverse-engineer a stable browser's bar and work exactly as it does.

**What loading means.** The engine had reported the frame tree's loading (`DidStartLoading` and `DidStopLoading`), which an iframe starts too: an embed or a widget loading as its tab comes forward, an ad refreshing. Chrome's own toolbar and tabs show `ShouldShowLoadingUI`, and so do Dia (its `isLoading` follows that state) and Aside (its bar starts on cross-document navigations of the main frame alone), as Safari and Chrome for Android do too. `TabBridge` (`pane/browser/ui/tab_bridge.mm`) reports that state at the frame tree's start and stop and at each of the main frame's progress reports. Together they cover every change: a main frame's load reports its progress as it starts and as it ends, each after its state changed. The fix changed the engine's key to `0d0a01f8c703` (ADR 0049); its archive replaced `engine-4c2b8172f926`, which was waiting to be published.

**The bar.** The reference is Chromium's own, read in the local checkout: Chrome for Android's `ToolbarProgressBar`, with its `LoadProgressMediator`, `ProgressAnimationSmooth`, and `ToolbarProgressBarAnimatingView`, as stable Chrome runs it (its composited animation, `AndroidAnimatedProgressBarInBrowser`, is off by default). `ToolbarProgress` (`app/PaneKit/Sources/PaneKit/ToolbarProgress.swift`) is the port, with Chrome's constants, and `ToolbarProgressBar` (`app/Pane/Views/Components/ToolbarProgressBar.swift`) draws it, on the display's frames while something moves.

A load shows its real progress at once, at least 5%, fading in over 140 ms (Material's linear-out-slow-in), and jumps to each new value. Five seconds with no news start the slow mode: a shimmer crosses the filled part, slow then fast, ln(width) × 260 ms a pass and a second apart, growing from 10% to 30% of the filled width, and the fill moves on a constant acceleration and deceleration of 0.15 a second squared. A finished load fills the bar (in the slow mode racing there at 7 a second squared), holds 100 ms, and fades out over 140 ms (fast-out-linear-in). A tab come forward with no load, a page's crash, or its page released hides the bar at once; a tab come forward mid-load shows its own progress.

The bar has followed the selected tab since a study of how Aside, Dia, Safari, and Chrome show a load (the web, and Aside's and Dia's binaries, read-only): a tab coming forward shows its own state at once, never an animation of the last one's, where the bar had played the last tab's finish (a fill and a fade) over a loaded tab. Dia keys on the frame tree's loading-UI state and draws its bar in Metal. Aside's bar lives in its `LocationBarView`: fake progress, 2% each 500 ms up to 90%, held a second at 100% before it fades, and a switch to a loaded tab hides it at once. Chrome for Android fills to 100%, holds 100 ms, and fades in 140 ms. Safari's starts at 10% and stays under 90% until the load is done.

The port replaced the Electron app's bar, 8 seconds of made-up progress to 90%, along with the 5% start and the resumed curve for a tab come forward that followed the study (`loadingSince`, gone). One difference is on purpose: Chrome's mediator keeps its last progress across loads, so a load starting at the value the last one ended at drew an empty bar until its next value; this one draws it. The look stays Pane's: the blue-500 to blue-400 gradient, 2 points under the address, and no track (Chrome's has one). While the Mac reduces motion, the fades are instant.

How it is checked:

- **`page-loading`** adds a 1.5-second iframe to a loaded page: the old engine reported the tab loading for all of it (14 samples of 14), the new one never. A tab coming forward shows as loaded, and a slow navigation still shows as loading from its start to its end. The background scenarios passed, 55 of them; the 8 that bring the app forward waited, the user's Pane being open.
- **Ten PaneKit tests**, one per rule of the bar.
- **`progress-bar`** holds a load 3 s at the server: the bar starts at 10%, a tab with no load hides it at once, the loading tab shows its 10% again, and the end fills the bar and fades it. The shimmer's start is `ToolbarProgressTests`', past 5 s. In captured frames, the finish in the slow mode filled in 0.46 s (7 a second squared predicts 0.51), held, and was gone 0.25 s later.
