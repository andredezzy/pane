# ⌘Q asks first

⌘Q asks before Pane quits, as Aside does (the user's request). Quit Pane?, designed in Paper first (`04 · Browsing` › 04.5 Quit Pane, light and dark, and Quit confirmation in `03 · Components` › 03.5 Overlays), sits over the window on a light veil, black 14% (45% on dark): a 40-point icon tile with a dashed circle-x, the title, what quitting does (profiles and tabs come back; downloads in progress stop), then Cancel with its Esc key and the destructive Quit with ⌘Q (`QuitDialog` in a `ModalDialog`, `pane/ui/window/quit_dialog.h`, `pane/ui/design/dialog.h`). It is made with the window, so ⌘Q makes nothing.

⌘Q again, Return, or Quit quits; Esc (with any modifier), Cancel, or a click outside goes back, and gives the keys back to what had them. While it shows it takes every key, as the PIN screen does, so none reaches the page whatever has focus: Esc and Return are its own, and the window drops any other key of the menu bar's but ⌘Q (`KeyEquivalentsForPane`, ADR 0019). At the PIN screen, Settings' too, ⌘Q quits at once: nothing is open to lose there. With no window open to ask in, ⌘Q quits at once too (`UiApp::ConfirmQuit`).

A Lock dismisses it, as Cancel does, so the PIN gives back the pages, not a question asked before the lock: the user's call (2026-11-11), where the Swift app drew the PIN screen over it and showed it again after the PIN (`QuitConfirmationView`, until 0.20.0).

**On Windows** (W4, 2026-11-13), Ctrl+Q and Alt+F4, Windows' own key for closing an app's window, ask first as ⌘Q does, and either again, or Return, quits; at the PIN screen they quit at once, past the screen that holds the keys (ADR 0019). The window's own close (its caption button, the system menu's Close, the taskbar's Close window) quits once its pages agree, without asking, as Windows' apps and Chrome there quit once their last window closes: the Mac's red button closes without asking too, and leaves Pane in the Dock (`ClosingTheWindowQuits`, `pane/ui/app/app_events.h`). Before, closing it left Pane running with no window and no way back but launching it again. Quit Pane? shows ⌘Q on its key there too until Paper draws Windows' (W5).

It replaces Chrome's own hold-to-quit. Chrome's Warn Before Quitting pref is on by default and asks for a held ⌘Q in a window of its own; the engine turns it off (`kConfirmToQuitEnabled`, `pane/browser/browser_main_extra_parts_mac.cc`). The `quit` scenario drives Quit Pane? through the app's real key path, ⌘Q posted into the app's queue, which takes the menu bar's real path, Esc giving the page its focus again, ⌘Q then Return quitting, and ⌘Q twice on the Dark theme; Esc and Return take the window's own path where the run lets Pane take the front (ADR 0012). `hosted` chooses Quit Pane twice.
