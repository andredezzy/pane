# The app saves its session when the engine shuts down

Session restore is the app's, as it was the Electron app's: Pane persists its own `profiles.json`, and saves it at `browserApplicationWillShutDown:`, rather than at `NSApplicationWillTerminateNotification`, which Chromium's quit path does not reliably post. The engine calls that optional delegate method once as it shuts down: as quitting starts, or after the message loop (`PostMainMessageLoopRun`) at the latest, before it releases the profiles. There the app's `saveNow` writes what changed and waits for the write (ADR 0068).

The session saved is the one the user left, because a closing window or a quitting engine does not report its tabs as removed. `WindowHost` skips `didRemoveTab` while a tab strip closes all its tabs, the window closes, or the engine quits; before, the app saw every tab leave at ⌘Q and saved empty profiles. `didRemoveTab` means "this tab closed", by the app once beforeunload agreed or by the page itself, and the tabs of a closing window or a quitting engine stay the app's. AppKit's own window restoration is off, since Pane restores its session itself (ADR 0028). `restore` checks that profiles and their tabs, in order, survive quitting and relaunching.
