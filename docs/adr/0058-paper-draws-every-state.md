# Paper draws every state the app shows, reviewed against the app

The Pane file in Paper, the design tool the user works in, is the design source: it draws every state the app shows, light and dark, and is checked against the app, so neither drifts unseen. It first documented the Electron app's design, which the native UI reproduces (ADR 0034). New UI is designed there first (Quit Pane?, the extensions menu, the extension prompts, the dmg's window: ADRs 0053, 0054, 0056, 0046), and a change made in the app is carried to every board that shows it. Where the two differ, Paper wins, unless Paper is the one out of date.

The Browsing page draws every state the window can show (the user's request), each light and dark, numbered after 03.6. The states come from the code (`RootView`'s overlays, `DialogPresenter`'s requests, `PageContextMenu`, `SadTabView`, `ExclusiveAccessHint`, and `UpdatePill`), in the app's own texts: the address focused; the extensions menu and an extension's more menu; adding and removing an extension; an extension's popup; a pinned action's right-click menu; the page's context menu; the JavaScript alert, confirm, and prompt; Leave site; form resubmission; a permission request; sign-in; opening an external app; an unresponsive page; a crashed page; page fullscreen with its Esc hint; docked DevTools; and the update pill downloading. They derive from 03.7 Page loaded. Only the file chooser, the system's own panel, is left out.

What macOS draws, Paper draws as macOS does. The page's sheets were copied from a real `NSAlert` sheet, captured in a test app's own window: 260 points wide, the title in bold, and capsule buttons, Cancel at #DADADA and the default at #3478F6. Those boards, 03.15 to 03.23, are what the app's own dialogs draw now (ADR 0080). The right-click menu must be the Mac's own (the user's word), as the app's always was: SwiftUI's `.contextMenu` is an `NSMenu`. 03.13 drew a flat card; it now copies the `NSMenu` in the user's Aside screenshot, measured at 2×: 286 points wide, radius 13, with 5 points above and below; 24-point rows, the text 16 points in; 11-point separators, their line 16 points in from each side; a half-point outline with a 1-point light rim inside it; and glass, a blur, over the page. Its text stays Inter, since SF Pro is not available to Paper.

Reviews keep the two together. Rendering each state from the app beside Paper's (the user's request) found Paper stale, and Paper was fixed: the toolbar's puzzle, missing in 02.4, 03.2 to 03.5, and the cover; Settings (05.1), with the Electron app's Browser section and version 0.6.0, and without the profile select, Update all, or each card's Pinned and Enabled switches; 02.9's more menu, still native; and the theme principle, which named Electron's `nativeTheme` there and in `DESIGN.md`. The app's profile sheet, spacing its fields 3 to 6 points wider than Paper's, is the app drifting from the design, and that pass left it as found. A review of the whole file (the user's request) put its pages in number order, the app's screens after the divider, gave every flow page its header, kept every page on its grid, and fixed six boards out of date. After the review fixes, checking every board against the app settled three older differences on Paper's side: fields start their text 13 points in, as CSS's `px-3` inside a 1-pixel border does (SwiftUI's border sat over the padding, so the app's started at 12); the Extensions page's search is 13 points, on Pane's scale, not 14; and a disabled extension dims its icon with its text, to 60%.
