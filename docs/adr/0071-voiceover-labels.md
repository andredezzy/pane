# VoiceOver names Pane's controls, reads their text, and stays in the modal layer on top

Pane's own controls read to VoiceOver by what they are and what they hold, not by their placeholders or by the text drawn over them, through Views' accessibility (`GetViewAccessibility()`), and VoiceOver reads only the layer of the window on top while a modal one shows.

**What each control reads.**

- **The sidebar** (`pane/ui/sidebar/`): a profile's row keeps its name as its accessible name and reads its state as its value ("3 tabs, collapsed, proxy connected", `profile_row.cc`), selected while its page shows; a tab's row reads its title, Loading without one, selected while shown, and its close button Close tab; the sidebar's edge is a splitter, Sidebar width, valued at the width and stepping by 10.
- **The toolbar** (`pane/ui/toolbar/`): the address's name sits on the field itself, and the domain and title drawn over it at rest read as text of their own: set on the whole view, the name renamed them (ADR 0060). Reload reads Stop while the page loads. The update pill's button reads its status, and its dismiss button Dismiss.
- **A page's dialog** reads as a modal dialog by its title, its fields by their placeholders (Text without one, the password's protected), and its buttons by their titles, the default marked (ADR 0021). An extension's prompt reads the same way, its accept marked the default, and Report abuse as a checkbox, checked or not (ADR 0056).
- **Find**: its field reads "Find in page" though it shows no placeholder (ADR 0067), its count its text, and its buttons Previous match, Next match, and Close find bar. Docked DevTools read "Developer tools", the page "Web content".
- **The extensions** (ADR 0054): the puzzle reads Extensions, selected while its menu shows; a pinned action its action's title, else its name, selected while its popup shows; the menu reads as the group Extensions, Installed extensions as a heading, each row by its title, a pin Pin to toolbar or Unpin from toolbar, selected while pinned, and More; the more menu as a group named by its extension. Chrome's extensions pages read as Chrome's.
- **The profile sheet** (ADR 0120) reads as a modal dialog, New profile or Edit profile, its controls by their labels in its focus order, with the identifiers the Swift app's had (`pane.profile.*`, `pane/ui/view_ids.cc`): the proxy's fields Proxy host and Proxy port, each switch by its title with its sentence as its description, the platform's tiles by their platform, selected when chosen. A problem is its field's description, announced when Save is refused.
- **The PIN screen** (ADR 0042) reads as a modal dialog (`pane.lockScreen`): its keys by their digits and Delete, the dots as "2 of 4 digits", the back arrow Back, Continue by its title, the attempts left as text, the data's move as "Encrypting your data" with its percent as its value, and the update's action by its title. A wrong PIN is announced with the attempts left.
- **The pickers** (`MenuButton`) read their label as their name and their option as their value ("Theme, System"), "menu pop up pop up button", as Chromium's own menu buttons do.

**Text under a view that holds the keys is exposed.** VoiceOver found what the names alone didn't show, in the front session with VoiceOver of 2026-11-12. Outside ChromeOS, Chromium leaves out every view that can't take the focus under one that can (`ViewAccessibility::UpdateIgnoredState`), and the PIN screen, Settings, the dialogs, an extension's prompt, and the sad tab hold the keys that way, so their text went: the PIN screen's dots, its error, and the attempts left, Settings' headings and descriptions, a page's `alert()` message, Quit Pane?'s, a prompt's permissions; a wrong PIN said nothing. Such text now takes the focus in accessibility mode alone (`ExposeToScreenReaders`, `pane/ui/design/text.h`), as Chromium's own dialogs expose their body text, and Settings' section labels read as headings. The sidebar's edge takes the focus in accessibility mode alone too, a splitter having its value only while focusable; VoiceOver still reads it "horizontal … collapsed on top", from an orientation and adjoining contents Chromium's Mac nodes for views don't expose. The sheet's time zone reads once, its note as its description.

**VoiceOver stays in the modal layer on top.** Under the PIN screen, a sheet, or a dialog, VoiceOver walked the rest of the window, and opened Settings' menus over the Lock (a choice made there was refused): Views leaves the rest of a window out only for a modal child window, not for a modal view in it. The root view leaves out each layer under the topmost modal one that shows, and each hidden one (`UpdateAccessibleLayers`, `pane/ui/design/accessible_layers.h`), which also ends a control of hidden Settings read beside the page (Chromium counts a view shown inside a hidden one as shown to its own children) and the hidden page under Settings. The Swift app kept VoiceOver on the PIN screen with SwiftUI's `.isModal`, and named its sheet's fields through a `LabeledField` of its own.

How it is checked:

- `pane_unittests`: `AccessibleLayersTest` (a modal layer leaves out what is under it; a hidden layer stays out with what shows in it) and `ScreenReaderTextTest` (text under a view holding the keys is read once exposed).
- `profile-sheet` reads a profile row's name and value, the sheet's role, name, and modality, and its controls' names and selection from Views' accessibility; it failed before.
- The rigs read the rest through the channel (`dialogAccessibility`, `findAccessibility`, `extensionsAccessibility`, `accessibility`): `tests/rigs/views-browsing.ts`, `views-dialogs.ts`, `views-extensions.ts`, `views-keys.ts`, and `views-profile-sheet.ts`; `views-lock.ts` reads the system's accessibility tree at the Lock.
- VoiceOver itself, through its AppleScript, walked the window, the Lock, the sheet, and the dialogs on 2026-11-12 (`docs/human-checklist.md`).
