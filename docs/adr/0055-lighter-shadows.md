# Shadows are lighter, one per surface

Each surface casts one shadow, from its own shape, and the shadows are lighter: the user reported a shadow inside a menu, then asked for lighter ones. The extensions menu and the tab switcher had put shadow-2xl on their whole view, and SwiftUI then shadows every view inside it, so each row, text, and fill cast its own: the hovered row cast a shadow inside the menu, and the switcher's selected row had shaded the panel under it since the switcher was built. The shadow is now on the surface's shape alone. Rendered before and after, the panel between rows is pure white (255, where it was 252 under the text), and the switcher's selected fill is even (241, where it ran 238 to 241).

The content panel's shadow is 0 2px 8px, black 8% (25% on dark), down from 0 4px 24px at 16% (40%), and the lock screen shares it. Popovers (the extensions menus, their popups, and the tab switcher) take 0 6px 20px, black 14%, as macOS's menus do, down from 0 25px 50px −12px at 25%. Both live in `app/Pane/Views/Theme/Elevation.swift`, as `surfaceBackground` and `popoverSurface`, where a CSS blur is twice the Core Animation shadow radius. Paper follows on every board (ADR 0058).
