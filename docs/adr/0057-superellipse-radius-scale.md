# Every corner is a superellipse on the design's radius scale

Every rounded corner in Pane is a superellipse (the user's request, for the whole design), and every radius is one of the scale's eight: 4, 7, 9, 11, 15, 18, 22, and 29 (the user's call, after asking which radius the design uses). `DESIGN.md` › Shape holds the scale and each radius's uses. In Paper, 1,049 rounded layers take `corner-shape: superellipse(2)`, CSS's squircle. In the app, every circular `RoundedRectangle` became `Squircle` (`app/Pane/Views/Theme/Squircle.swift`), the same curve: in each corner's radius box, the quarter of |x|⁴ + |y|⁴ = 1. Circles and pills keep their round ends (in Paper, 225 layers at 50%, 13 at full, and the bars whose radius is half their width), and the 25 artboards that stand for macOS windows keep their corners. The 4-by-14 profile bars are `Capsule`s, as they were in effect. The corners had been circular, as CSS draws them (ADR 0034).

Apple's continuous corners were not the answer: SwiftUI's `.continuous` at a radius stays within 0.014 of it from a circle, so switching to it changes nothing visible, while the superellipse at the same radius is up to 0.19 of it squarer. At the user's next word ("aumente o radius"), every radius grew 1.84×, which keeps a corner as deep at 45° as the circle was: 2 → 4, 4 → 7, 5 → 9, 6 → 11, 8 → 15, 9 → 17, 10 → 18, 12 → 22, 16 → 29. Paper's radius tokens follow.

Five values had drifted from the scale since, and moved onto it in the app and in Paper: a page's dialogs 26 → 29, as Quit Pane? and an extension's prompt; their fields 6 → 7; the find bar 14 → 15 (Aside's circle of 8 is 14.7); and a toolbar action's letter tile 5 → 4, a favicon's. `hoverBackground`'s default of 5, which no caller used, is gone. Paper's `--radius-*` tokens carry the same uses as `DESIGN.md`, with `--radius-xs` (4) and `--radius-2xl` (29) added. In Paper, 18 dialogs, their 6 fields, and the 5 find bars moved; the dialogs' 36 buttons stay capsules, as the app draws them. Renders of the prompt dialog (29, its field 7, capsule buttons) and the find bar (15) check it.

Three corners stand apart. A ring stays even around its shape by taking the shape's radius grown by its offset, through the circle (`Squircle.circularEquivalent`): the profile sheet's selected swatch, 11, has its rings at 15 and 17. An extension's popup is AppKit's `NSPopover`, whose corner macOS draws; Paper's circular 14 shows it. The page view is a layer, whose corners are only circular or continuous: a circle of 0.57 of the radius follows the superellipse within 0.012 of it (0.2 pixels at the content panel's 18), so the page keeps a plain corner radius (10.3) and no mask composites it each frame.
