# Every corner is a superellipse on the design's radius scale

> **Refined by [ADR 0064](0064-active-profile-indicator.md)** — the profile bars are squircles that morph, no longer capsules. **[ADR 0089](0089-app-icon-is-a-window-the-user-picks-its-sky.md)** adds a corner apart: the App icon's ring follows the system icon's continuous corner. The rest stands.

Every rounded corner in Pane is a superellipse (the user's request, for the whole design), and every radius is one of the scale's eight: 4, 7, 9, 11, 15, 18, 22, and 29 (the user's call, after asking which radius the design uses). `DESIGN.md` › Shape holds the scale and each radius's uses. In Paper, 1,049 rounded layers take `corner-shape: superellipse(2)`, CSS's squircle. In the app, every corner is `SquirclePath` (`pane/ui/design/squircle.h`, its radii `radius::kXs` to `radius::k2xl`), the same curve: in each corner's radius box, the quarter of |x|⁴ + |y|⁴ = 1, each corner two cubics as Blink draws one. The Swift app's every circular `RoundedRectangle` had become its `Squircle` shape. Circles and pills keep their round ends (in Paper, 225 layers at 50%, 13 at full, and the bars whose radius is half their width), and the 25 artboards that stand for macOS windows keep their corners. The profile bars, 4-by-14 `Capsule`s at first, are 4 by 16 now, squircles of radius 2, half their width, and the active one's grows into a 16-point square of 4 (ADR 0064). The corners had been circular, as CSS draws them (ADR 0034).

Apple's continuous corners were not the answer: SwiftUI's `.continuous` at a radius stays within 0.014 of it from a circle, so switching to it changes nothing visible, while the superellipse at the same radius is up to 0.19 of it squarer. At the user's next word ("aumente o radius"), every radius grew 1.84×, which keeps a corner as deep at 45° as the circle was: 2 → 4, 4 → 7, 5 → 9, 6 → 11, 8 → 15, 9 → 17 (no corner keeps 17 now but the swatch's outer ring), 10 → 18, 12 → 22, 16 → 29. Paper's radius tokens follow.

Five values had drifted from the scale since, and moved onto it in the app and in Paper: a page's dialogs 26 → 29, as Quit Pane? and an extension's prompt; their fields 6 → 7; the find bar 14 → 15 (Aside's circle of 8 is 14.7); and a toolbar action's letter tile 5 → 4, a favicon's. Paper's `--radius-*` tokens carry the same uses as `DESIGN.md`, with `--radius-xs` (4) and `--radius-2xl` (29) added. In Paper, 18 dialogs, their 6 fields, and the 5 find bars moved; the dialogs' 36 buttons stay capsules, as the app draws them. Renders of the prompt dialog (29, its field 7, capsule buttons) and the find bar (15) check it.

Five corners stand apart. A ring stays even around its shape by taking the shape's radius grown by its offset, through the circle (0.57 of the radius, `kCircularEquivalent`): the profile sheet's selected swatch, 11, has its rings at 15 and 17. The 1-point rings take the radius plus 1 instead: a focused field's 12 around its 11, and the switcher's selected row's 16 around its 15. The App icon's tiles ring the chosen icon on the system icon's own corner, continuous at 0.257 of its side (ADR 0089). An extension's popup keeps the circular 14 macOS gave `NSPopover`, which Paper 04.12 draws, the page inside it at 9: it is Pane's own bubble, drawn to that board by the user's answer on Paper 10.5 (`pane/ui/extensions/action_popup.cc`, ADR 0031). The page view is a layer, whose corners are only circular or continuous: a circle of 0.57 of the radius follows the superellipse within 0.012 of it (0.2 points, 0.4 pixels at 2×, at the content panel's 18), so the page keeps a plain corner radius (10.3, `kPanelLayerRadius`) and no mask composites it each frame (ADR 0073). Two shadows take the layers' rule too, since Pane's views raster on the CPU (ADR 0123): Quit Pane?'s and the App icon picker's (a Gaussian of 32) and the tab switcher's (of 10) are cast by a rect rounded at 0.57 of the radius, where their card and panel stay squircles. Blurred that wide, the two shapes' shadows are within a 255th, and a rounded rect is the shape Skia blurs once rather than for every tile.
