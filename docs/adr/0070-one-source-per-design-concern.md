# The view layer has one source per concern

Each design concern in the app's views has one source that every view uses (the colors, the shapes, the shadows, the icons, the motion, and one style per kind of control), so controls of one kind look and behave alike and a change lands once. The view layer's review made it so, under the user's standing order to fix everything found, and rendered every surface before and after, light and dark (the automation's `render`, and screen captures where a mask or the page matters).

The former `Theme.swift` and `Style.swift` are split by concern. `app/Pane/Views/Theme/` holds the palette (`Palette.swift`), the squircle (`Squircle.swift`, ADR 0057), the elevations (`Elevation.swift`, ADR 0055), the icons (`Icons.swift`), and `Motion` (`Motion.swift`). `app/Pane/Views/Controls/` holds the buttons, fields, menus, switch, labels, hairline, and hover fill, and `app/Pane/Views/Components/` the favicon, the fading scroll view, and since then the reorder and the window's drag area (ADR 0077).

Every color is Palette's, including the status colors (`warning`, `success`, `failure`, `successFill`), the letter tile and the badge's ring, the modal scrim, the dialogs' shadow, and the window's background (`windowBackground`, the NSColor the window and SwiftUI share). One hex initialiser makes colors: `Color(hex:)` parses, then calls `Color(rgb:)`. Separators are one `Hairline`, each in its own color as before. Icons are Lucide's only. The Extensions page's search icon, an SF Symbol at 13, is Lucide's `search` (lucide-react 0.542's paths) at 15 points, with the same 12.5 points of ink; the profile select's chevron, an SF Symbol too, is Lucide's at 16.

Every animation goes through `Motion`, named for what it is (`hover`, `toggle`, `morph`, `slide`, `drop`, `panel`, `sheet`, `lockIn`, `lockOut`, `fade`, `status`, `progress`, and since then `reveal`, ADR 0084), and each is nil while the Mac reduces motion, read from `NSWorkspace` as the change happens. No reduce-motion read is left outside `Motion`: Quit Pane?'s fade and the dialogs' are `Motion.panel`, and the action popover's animation reads `Motion.isReduced`. Eighteen copies of the 0.15-second literal went, and the animations that ignored the setting honor it now: the PIN dots' fill, the lock screen's update hover, the update pill's hover, status, and progress, the pill entering the footer, the reorder springs, and the update dot's pulse. The proxy test's spinner keeps turning, as the system's do: it is the only sign of the work going on.

Each kind of button has one style, which owns its hover, press, and disabled look. `IconButtonStyle` draws every icon button (back, forward, reload, Collapse all, the puzzle, the pinned actions, find's chevrons and close, the PIN screen's back, and the rest), `RowButtonStyle` the sidebar's rows, and `PaneButtonStyle` every text button, with Aside's popup buttons (`.popup(isPrimary:)`) and the dialogs' keyed ones (`.keyed(role)`) as its sizes and options. Hover is one state set inside `withAnimation(Motion.hover)`, so a row's text and a tab's close X fade with its fill instead of jumping; the X reads the row's hover from the environment (`isRowHovered`). A press lays the foreground at 6% over the fill (the card at 10% over a primary or destructive one) in the next frame, and fades out as it lets go. Only the press layer animates, so the click's own effect, a selection, lands at once; the switch's track takes the press too. Disabled icon buttons are 40% and never light, even when hovered as they turned off. Rendered at rest, disabled, and with hover forced on, in both builds, the find bar's buttons, the PIN screen's back, Quit Pane?, the install dialog, and the remove popup look as before, light and dark. The keyed label keeps its own size: under the style's one-line limit, the chip cut ⌘Q to an ellipsis.

One menu trigger, `PaneMenuButton`, serves the profile sheet's proxy type, Settings' pop-ups, and the Extensions page's profile select: the value and its icon, a select's chevron or Aside's up and down one, a border in any color, the accent's fill on hover, and the system's menu with the current option checked. Settings' pop-ups render as before. The proxy type and the profile select gained the hover fill and the checked option, their chevron muted and 10 points from the end (12 before).
