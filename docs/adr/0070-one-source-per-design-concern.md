# The view layer has one source per concern

> **On the Views line** (`docs/plans/views-migration.md`, H3): a spring that takes over a change under way starts as fast as it was moving, as SwiftUI merges one spring into another, so a dragged row heading for a new place never stops dead (`Curve::At`, `ValueMotion`, `pane/ui/design/motion.cc`); the three springs are each worked out once, where each use had worked its settle time out again (0.15 ms).

> **On the Views line** (`docs/plans/views-migration.md`, H4): a field's selection has one source, the system's (`kPaneColorTextSelection`, AppKit's colors in the appearance shown, following the user's Highlight color), dimmed to its unemphasized one while the window isn't key (`SelectionFollowsWindow`), as the Swift's text views were, where Chromium's own ended on Material's tonal container in dark; the update dot's pulse is one more curve (`motion::Pulse`).

> **On the Views line** (`docs/plans/views-migration.md`, H6): the app's one menu of options is `MenuButton` (`pane/ui/design/menu_button.cc`), and a dialog over the window is `ModalDialog` with `DialogHeading` and `KeyedButton` (`pane/ui/design/dialog.cc`, `text_button.cc`): the App icon picker's first, Quit Pane?'s next (H7).

> **On the Views line** (`docs/plans/views-migration.md`, H7): the motion driver starts a change's clock a frame back (`FirstFrameStart`), so its first frame shows a frame's progress, as Core Animation's did, where it showed the start again and the change answered a frame late (Quit Pane? 21.2 to 14.2 ms to the frame on screen); a dialog's scrim is a color the compositor draws and its shadow a layer sized to its reach; `KeyedButton` takes the keyed roles, Cancel's and the destructive Quit's.

Each design concern in the app's views has one source that every view uses (the colors, the shapes, the shadows, the icons, the motion, and one style per kind of control), so controls of one kind look and behave alike and a change lands once. A review of the view layer made it so, and rendered every surface before and after, light and dark (the automation's `render`, and screen captures where a mask or the page matters).

The former `Theme.swift` and `Style.swift` are split by concern. `app/Pane/Views/Theme/` holds the palette (`Palette.swift`), the squircle (`Squircle.swift`, ADR 0057), the elevations (`Elevation.swift`, ADR 0055), the icons (`Icons.swift`), and `Motion` (`Motion.swift`). `app/Pane/Views/Controls/` holds the buttons, fields, menus, switch, labels, hairline, and hover fill, and `app/Pane/Views/Components/` the favicon, the fading scroll view, and since then the reorder and the window's drag area (ADR 0077) and the toolbar's progress bar (ADR 0081).

Every color is Palette's, including the status colors (`warning`, `success`, `failure`), the letter tile and the badge's ring, the modal scrim, the dialogs' shadow, and the window's background (`windowBackground`, the NSColor the window and SwiftUI share). One hex initialiser makes colors: `Color(hex:)` parses, then calls `Color(rgb:)`. Separators are one `Hairline`, each in its own color as before. Icons are Lucide's only. The Extensions page's search icon, an SF Symbol at 13, is Lucide's `search` (lucide-react 0.542's paths) at 15 points, with the same 12.5 points of ink; the profile select's chevron, an SF Symbol too, is Lucide's at 16.

Every animation goes through `Motion`, named for what it is (`hover`, `toggle`, `morph`, `slide`, `drop`, `panel`, `sheet`, `lockIn`, `lockOut`, `fade`, `status`, `progress`, and since then `reveal`, ADR 0084, and `hoverTurn`, `statusAfterBar`, and `barExit`, ADR 0092), and each is nil while the Mac reduces motion, read from `NSWorkspace` as the change happens. No reduce-motion read is left outside `Motion`: Quit Pane?'s fade and the dialogs' are `Motion.panel`, and the action popover's animation reads `Motion.isReduced`. Eighteen copies of the 0.15-second literal went, and the animations that ignored the setting honor it now: the PIN dots' fill, the lock screen's update hover, the update pill's hover, status, and progress, the pill entering the footer, the reorder springs, and the update dot's pulse. The proxy test's spinner kept turning while it lasted, as the system's do; it went with the profile sheet's redesign (ADR 0120).

Each kind of button has one style, which owns its hover, press, and disabled look. `IconButtonStyle` draws every icon button (back, forward, reload, Collapse all, the puzzle, the pinned actions, find's chevrons and close, the PIN screen's back, and the rest), `RowButtonStyle` the sidebar's rows, and `PaneButtonStyle` every text button, with Aside's popup buttons (`.popup(isPrimary:)`) and the dialogs' keyed ones (`.keyed(role)`) as its sizes and options. Hover is one state set inside `withAnimation(Motion.hover)`, so a row's text and a tab's close X fade with its fill instead of jumping; the X reads the row's hover from the environment (`isRowHovered`). A press lays the foreground at 6% over the fill (the card at 10% over a primary or destructive one) in the next frame, and fades out as it lets go. Only the press layer animates, so the click's own effect, a selection, lands at once; the switch's track takes the press too. Disabled icon buttons are 40% and never light, even when hovered as they turned off. Rendered at rest, disabled, and with hover forced on, in both builds, the find bar's buttons, the PIN screen's back, Quit Pane?, the install dialog, and the remove popup look as before, light and dark. The keyed label keeps its own size: under the style's one-line limit, the chip cut ⌘Q to an ellipsis.

One menu trigger, `PaneMenuButton`, serves the profile sheet's proxy type, Settings' pop-ups, and the Extensions page's profile select: the value and its icon, a select's chevron or Aside's up and down one, a border in any color, the accent's fill on hover, and the system's menu with the current option checked. Settings' pop-ups render as before. The proxy type and the profile select gained the hover fill and the checked option, their chevron muted and 10 points from the end (12 before).
