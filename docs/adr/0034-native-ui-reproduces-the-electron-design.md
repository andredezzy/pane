# The native UI reproduces the Electron app's design, as Paper documents it

> **Refined by later records**, each moving one first choice: corners are superellipses ([ADR 0057](0057-superellipse-radius-scale.md)); the sidebar and the toolbar take Aside's sizes, their text at 12 points ([ADR 0060](0060-sidebar-and-toolbar-sizes.md)), and the sidebar resizes ([ADR 0061](0061-resizable-sidebar.md)); shadows are lighter, one per surface ([ADR 0055](0055-lighter-shadows.md)); outline controls have no fill ([ADR 0063](0063-outline-controls.md)); the active profile's bar becomes a square and its moon is gone ([ADR 0064](0064-active-profile-indicator.md)); Extensions left Settings for a page of their own ([ADR 0062](0062-extensions-page.md)), and Settings take Aside's layout ([ADR 0065](0065-settings-layout.md)); the profile sheet is 400 points wide, with its settings in cards ([ADR 0120](0120-profile-sheet-cards-and-platform-logos.md)); the find bar is Aside's ([ADR 0067](0067-find-bar.md)); the uninstall confirmation gave way to the extensions' removal popup ([ADR 0056](0056-extension-prompts.md)); the address field's loading bar is Chrome's toolbar progress bar ([ADR 0081](0081-loading-means-a-new-main-frame-document.md)); a page's dialogs, left as system sheets at first since the Electron app had none of its own, are drawn in the window ([ADR 0021](0021-chrome-ui-as-requests.md)); and the icon is a window, no longer the Electron app's ([ADR 0089](0089-app-icon-is-a-window-the-user-picks-its-sky.md)). The rest stands.

The native app's UI reproduces the Electron app's design, since the native app replaces it (ADR 0004), and the reference is the Electron app's renderer as the Pane file in Paper documents it: pages `02 · Foundations` to `07 · Lock screen`, with the tokens, spacing, radii, type, elevation, and every screen in light and dark. Paper, the design tool the user works in, is the design source, and later changes are drawn there first (ADR 0058).

The match was measured. Paper was read through the Paper app's local MCP server, read-only (screenshots, styles, tokens). The Electron app was measured live, its built bundle run on a throwaway data directory with remote debugging and its element boxes read with `getBoundingClientRect`, and the native app's frames were read from its accessibility tree. The two agreed to the point on the sidebar, the toolbar, the page area, Settings, and the profile sheet, with pixel diffs of each screen in both themes.

The design layer started from the Electron app's own: its CSS tokens as light and dark pairs, its lucide icons drawn from their own SVG paths (ISC, `third_party/lucide/`), and packages/ui's Button, Input, Switch, and Separator with the same variants and sizes. It now lives, split by concern, in `app/Pane/Views/Theme/` and `app/Pane/Views/Controls/` (ADR 0070). The views reproduce CSS's own details: line boxes (text-sm is a 20px line, and an inline label in a 16px block takes a 24px line, where its margin does not apply), border-box widths (a 1px border widens a chip or an outline button by 2px), collapsing margins (the New tab row sits 4px under the last tab, not 8), box-shadows drawn outside the element, and `flex-1` sharing width before borders.

Surfaces sit as the Electron app's do: the toolbar inside the content panel, with the page 43px down it, where Electron's view sits; the profile chip; the profile sheet as a 360px side panel over a black 40% scrim; find at the window's top right; and the switcher's 320px panel. One detail follows Paper over the Electron app: unloaded tabs are at 60% opacity, Paper's TabItem spec. The Electron app sets the class, but its drag code's inline `opacity: 1` overrides it, so its unloaded tabs never dim. What the Electron app never had, such as each profile's own extension management, is drawn in the same language.

What the native app lacked at first came in Phase 5, as the Electron app had it: the Memory & disk, Security, and Updates settings, the profile sheet's fingerprint and proxy fields, the update pill, the proxy dot, and the lock screen. Settings and the profile sheet hold and do what the Electron app's do, with two departures: Settings › Browser, the Chrome path, went with the Google fallback it served (ADR 0039), and Keep loaded stays in the profile menu, where the Electron app has it. The fingerprint editor follows `profile-sheet.tsx`: a profile on the Mac's platform claims the Mac's GPU, a rename keeps the stored fingerprint, a platform change regenerates it, and the cross-OS note reads as the Electron app wrote it, with the platform since spelled "macOS" (ADR 0058). The Mac's GPU comes from Chrome's GPU info (`PaneBrowserApplication.hostWebGLVendor` and `hostWebGLRenderer`), not a WebGL context in the app; where the GPU info has the OS version, ANGLE gives WebGL "Unspecified Version", and the engine swaps it the same way, which a scenario compares with live WebGL. A fingerprint change on an open profile releases its pages and loads the selected one again, and the engine shuts down the profile's spare renderers, which started with the old one, so pages start in renderers that carry the new fingerprint (ADR 0038).
