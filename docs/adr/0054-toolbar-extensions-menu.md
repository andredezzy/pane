# The toolbar's extensions menu and pinned actions follow Aside's

The toolbar's extensions menu and its pinned actions are Aside's (the user's request), measured from Aside at 2× and designed in Paper first (`02 · Components` › 02.9 Browser · Extensions, and `03 · Browsing` › 03.6 Extensions menu). They replaced a SwiftUI `Menu` that listed each extension twice, then Pin or Unpin for each, and Manage Extensions….

Aside's menu is native, with no web page to read, so its icons were measured in a screenshot and redrawn on Lucide's grid: the puzzle (a knob on top, a notch on the right), a bell-shaped pin, the pin struck through, the arrow onto a line, and three dots (`Glyph` in `app/Pane/Views/Theme/Icons.swift`). The puzzle shows whenever a profile is open, even with no extension installed, after the pinned actions. At 2 of 24 it was too heavy (the user's report): in the toolbar it draws 1.4 of 24 at 20 points, 1.17 points, as the navigation icons then drew 2 of 24 at 14 (rendered at 2×, its edge carried 2.34 pixels of ink, the back arrow's shaft 2.32), and in menus the other icons' 1.75 of 24 at 16.

The menu sits under the puzzle, its trailing edge on the button's, on the tab switcher's surface: the popover color, a 10% ring, and the popovers' shadow (ADR 0055). It is 300 points wide, its rows 32 points tall, with a 16-point icon, a 13-point label, and a 24-point pin and more at the trailing edge. Manage all extensions (ADR 0062) and Visit Chrome Web Store come first. The second, under Chrome's own label, opens the store in a new tab of the profile, whose install button installs into it; it fills the slot of Aside's Import extensions, which brings extensions from other browsers, as Pane cannot. Installed extensions follows, with the profile's enabled extensions, or No installed extensions.

A row runs its extension's action, the popup pointing at the puzzle when the extension is not pinned. The pin pins and unpins with the menu open, blue while pinned. More opens the more menu, Pane's own as Aside's is (ADR 0056), 240 points beside the menu and level with the row: the name (its Web Store page), Remove from Pane, Pin or Unpin, Manage Extension (the Extensions page, at its card), and Options when there is a page. Esc, a click outside, another tab, profile, or page, or the window losing key status closes the menu.

A pinned action's right-click menu is native, as Aside's: no icons, Title Case, the name (the Web Store page), Remove from Pane and Unpin, then Manage Extension. Aside's also lists the extension's own action items (`chrome.contextMenus` in the action's context), This Can Read and Change Site Data, and View Web Permissions. Paper draws them, but they need the engine to expose the action's menu items and site access, so the app does not show them yet. Options, which Aside leaves out there, stays in the more menu.

Pinned icons are 16 points, as Chrome and Aside draw action icons; the Electron app's 19.6 (70% of a 28-point button) were too large (the user's report). An extension without an icon shows its letter, 9 points on a 16-point tile. The pinned actions sit in a capsule, as Aside's (the user's request): 32-point circles, Aside's size since the toolbar took it (ADR 0060), 4 apart and flush inside its 1-point border, the puzzle outside. The capsule shows only while the pointer is over them or one's popup is open, as Aside's has none at rest. A circle fills with the accent on hover, while its popup shows (`ExtensionPopover` is observed for it), and while its right-click menu is open, as Aside's 1Password button does.

The `extensions-menu` scenario presses each control. Run in the background, it uses the menu after the tab an install opens (Dark Reader's help page), which gives the window key status and takes it back, closing the menu. A click outside needs the key window, which another app keeps while someone works on the Mac; the scenario then records that check as not run and closes the menu with Esc.
