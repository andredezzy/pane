# The toolbar's extensions menu and pinned actions follow Aside's

> **Its right-click menu's missing action items and site access rows ship, by [ADR 0096](0096-extension-menu-items-show-where-chrome-shows-them.md)**: a pinned action's right-click menu lists the extension's own action items, This Can Read and Change Site Data (or Can't Read or Change Site's Data), and View Web Permissions. **Manage Extension opens the extension's Details, in both menus, by [ADR 0062](0062-extensions-page.md)**, rather than the Extensions page at its card. The rest stands.

The toolbar's extensions menu and its pinned actions are Aside's (the user's request), measured from Aside at 2× and designed in Paper first (`03 · Components` › 03.9 Browser · Extensions, and `04 · Browsing` › 04.6 Extensions menu). They are Pane's views (`pane/ui/extensions/`): the pinned actions and the puzzle (`ExtensionActionsView`), in the toolbar's 32-point place 4 after the address, the menu and the more menu (`ExtensionsMenuView`, `ExtensionMoreMenuView`), on the popovers' look (`DialogCard`). They replaced a SwiftUI `Menu` that listed each extension twice, then Pin or Unpin for each, and Manage Extensions…; the Swift app drew them as SwiftUI views until 0.20.0.

Aside's menu is native, with no web page to read, so its icons were measured in a screenshot and redrawn on Lucide's grid: the puzzle (a knob on top, a notch on the right), a bell-shaped pin, the pin struck through, the arrow onto a line, and three dots (`pane/ui/design/icons.h`). The puzzle shows whenever a profile is open, even with no extension installed, after the pinned actions. At 2 of 24 it was too heavy (the user's report): in the toolbar it draws 1.4 of 24 at 20 points, 1.17 points, as the navigation icons then drew 2 of 24 at 14 (rendered at 2×, its edge carried 2.34 pixels of ink, the back arrow's shaft 2.32), and in menus the other icons' 1.75 of 24 at 16.

The menu sits under the puzzle, its trailing edge on the button's, on the popovers' surface: the popover color, rounded 22, a 10% ring, and the popovers' shadow (ADR 0055). It is 300 points wide, its rows 32 points tall, with a 16-point icon, a 13-point label, and a 24-point pin and more at the trailing edge. Manage all extensions, which opens Chrome's own page, chrome://extensions, in the profile's tab (ADR 0062), and Visit Chrome Web Store come first. The second, under Chrome's own label, opens the store in a new tab of the profile, whose install button installs into it; it fills the slot of Aside's Import extensions, which brings extensions from other browsers, as Pane cannot. Installed extensions follows, with the profile's enabled extensions, or No installed extensions.

A row runs its extension's action, the popup pointing at the puzzle when the extension is not pinned. The pin pins and unpins with the menu open, blue while pinned. More opens the more menu, Pane's own as Aside's is, 240 points beside the menu, over its edge by 10, and level with the row: the name (its Web Store page), Remove from Pane, which asks in the popup under the extension's button (ADR 0056), Pin or Unpin, Manage Extension (the extension's Details, chrome://extensions/?id=, ADR 0062), and Options when there is a page. Esc, a click outside, another tab, profile, or page, or the window leaving the front closes the menu.

A pinned action's right-click menu is the system's, through `views::MenuRunner` (`pane/ui/extensions/action_menu.h`), as Aside's: no icons, Title Case, the name (the Web Store page), Remove from Pane and Unpin, then Manage Extension. Aside's also lists the extension's own action items (`chrome.contextMenus` in the action's context), This Can Read and Change Site Data, and View Web Permissions; Paper drew them, and they waited for the engine to expose the action's menu items and site access, which ADR 0096 shipped. Options, which Aside leaves out there, stays in the more menu.

Pinned icons are 16 points, as Chrome and Aside draw action icons; the Electron app's 19.6 (70% of a 28-point button) were too large (the user's report). An extension without an icon shows its letter, 9 points on a 16-point tile. A badge whose color the extension left unset is red under white text, as Chrome draws it; the Swift app's bridge had drawn it transparent. The pinned actions sit in a capsule, as Aside's (the user's request): 32-point circles, Aside's size since the toolbar took it (ADR 0060), 4 apart and flush inside its 1-point border, the puzzle outside. The capsule shows only while the pointer is over them or one's popup is open, as Aside's has none at rest. A circle fills with the accent on hover, while its popup shows, and while its right-click menu is open, as Aside's 1Password button does.

The `extensions-menu` scenario presses each control, each a real click at its view's middle through the channel, in the background too: Manage all extensions, Visit Chrome Web Store, a row's action, its pin with the menu open, its more menu with Manage Extension and Remove from Pane, and Esc and a click outside closing the menu.
